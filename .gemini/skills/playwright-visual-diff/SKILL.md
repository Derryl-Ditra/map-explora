# Skill: Playwright Visual Diff (Visual Auditor)
Source: microsoft/playwright-mcp

## Purpose
Captures before/after screenshots of the UI. Verifies that the Plus Jakarta Sans font and blue accents remain consistent across different screen sizes.

## Core Instructions
1. **Automated Verification**: Use Playwright to capture screenshots at multiple breakpoints (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop).
2. **Visual Consistency**: Compare screenshots against a baseline to detect unintended styling regressions.
3. **Cross-Platform Audit**: Ensure that the Dark Gray 95% background and high-contrast typography maintain legibility across different browser engines.
4. **Font/Accent Check**: Specifically verify the rendering of Plus Jakarta Sans and #3B82F6 blue accents.
