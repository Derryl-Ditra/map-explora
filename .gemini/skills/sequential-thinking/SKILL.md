# Skill: Sequential Thinking (Reasoning Engine)
Source: modelcontextprotocol/server-sequential-thinking

## Purpose
Forces a multi-step "thought trace" before writing code. Prevents rushing into infrastructure; forces planning the RaaS (Result as a Service) layout first.

## Core Instructions
1. **Reflective Problem Solving**: Break down complex problems into smaller, manageable steps.
2. **Dynamic Revision**: Constantly re-evaluate previous thoughts as new information arrives.
3. **Branching Logic**: Explore alternative paths and choose the most optimal one based on RaaS constraints.
4. **Validation**: Ensure each step of the reasoning is logically sound before proceeding to the next.

## MCP Configuration
- Command: `npx`
- Args: `["-y", "@modelcontextprotocol/server-sequential-thinking"]`
