# 20 November 2026: Lippo Mall Puri vs Central Park naming check

Owner flagged possible confusion between Lippo Mall and Central Park, suggesting "Central Park 1 or 2 is the new version of Lippo Mall".

Findings (public sources, 20 Nov 2026):
- Central Park 2 is the renamed Neo Soho mall, the second building of the Central Park Mall complex in Tanjung Duren, West Jakarta (Agung Podomoro). Rename took effect 22 November 2025. Source: Kompas, 13 Sep 2025 (https://travel.kompas.com/read/2025/09/13/050000927/mal-neo-soho-ganti-nama-jadi-mal-central-park-2-apa-yang-berubah). Official mall directory lists CENTRAL PARK and CENTRAL PARK 2 sections: https://www.centralparkjakarta.com/MapDir
- Lippo Mall Puri is a different mall in Puri Indah, Kembangan, West Jakarta (Lippo Group), still operating: https://www.lippomallpuri.com/banner/jam-operasional
- Official Rockstar Academy locations page lists both branches separately: "Central Park 2 (Neo Soho), Floor 3rd, Unit #L3-101A" and "Lippo Mall Puri": https://www.rockstaracademy.com/schedules-and-locations

Conclusion: the rename was Neo Soho -> Central Park 2, not Lippo Mall -> Central Park. Data updated: branch name "Central Park 2 (Neo Soho)", notes on both Rockstar entries record the relationship and sources.
