## Result

These are one provider's four branches, not four new brands. Each has its own official brochure, a checked Maps pin and operating-location evidence. The provider's Instagram-linked Linktree publishes all four brochures. A separate official post dated 5 November 2026 names all four pools. [1-7]

The brochures explicitly assign baby classes to 3-18 months and toddler classes to 18-36 months, with 30-minute lessons. These exact branch sources replace the partner's rounded 0-3-year template, which is not provider age evidence. The overlap at 18 months is preserved, with advice to confirm the class boundary. No 0-2-month eligibility is invented. [3-6, 8]

The proposal adds eight branch-specific program-age entries and four priced venues. No existing record is removed or changed. All 156 baseline records were compared against the downloaded main archive and remained value-equivalent.

## Price scope

Every branch-specific brochure states:

- Group baby/toddler package: Rp800,000 for four 30-minute lessons, 2-5 children per coach.
- Private baby/toddler package: Rp1,200,000 for four 30-minute lessons, or Rp2,100,000 for eight.
- Registration: an additional Rp150,000, separate from the lesson package.
- Pool entry and two companions are included. A parent must accompany the child inside the pool.

Each price row is official-listed and cites that branch's actual brochure, checked 18 November. Brochures have no visible publication date or price expiry. Package expiry, current timetable, remaining places and cancellation/refund conditions are not established, so the public note tells parents to confirm them before paying. [3-6]

The Rp250,000 holiday offer and December/January dates in the 5 November post are not assigned to baby/toddler classes. They are not used for these prices or schedules. The partner's Rp150,000/Rp600,000 leads have unclear units and lose to the explicit branch brochures rather than being blended. [7-8]
