## Trend method

Current 3 months vs prior 3 months, inside a window of up to 6 months for smaller branches. A trend label is stored only when the minimum is met: 10+ dated reviews in the window AND 3+ in each 3-month half. Anything below the minimum is stored as "insufficient" and the card shows "Not enough recent reviews to judge a trend."

Result: 7 improving, 31 steady, 8 worsening, 98 insufficient.

Dates stay month precision. Google shows relative labels ("3 weeks ago", "5 months ago"), so evidence bounds are ISO months marked approximate, never invented exact days. Labels of 6 months or older fall outside both halves. The UI re-verifies every stored label through the guard in reviewPulse.ts before showing it (method "maps-window-3m-3m").

## Complaint themes

Themes come from real customer comment text only (3 stars or lower, top 3 recurring keywords per branch). Owner responses never count as customer reviews.
